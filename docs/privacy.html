Privacy Policy — Heirloom

Honest photo restoration · for your family, not your followers

Effective: June 27, 2026 · Last updated: July 24, 2026

The short version: Heirloom restores one photo at a time. The photo you choose is sent over an encrypted connection, restored in temporary server storage, and returned to your phone. The server copy is deleted when the request finishes. We do not keep a cloud photo library, require an account, show ads, sell personal data, or use photos for AI training.

This Privacy Policy applies to Heirloom for Android ("the App"), developed by George Abrahamyants ("we," "us," "our").

What the App Does

Heirloom repairs old, torn, faded, or damaged family photos. You pick a photo; the App sends it to our restoration service, which mends damage, restores the face, sharpens, checks that the restored face still matches the original person, and (for black-and-white photos) adds color. The restored image is returned to you. That's the whole product.

What Data We Process

The photo you choose

When you tap to restore a photo, that single image is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection through Cloudflare to our restoration backend on Google Cloud. The service uses an isolated temporary working directory while the models run. That directory, including the original and restored files, is deleted automatically when the request finishes. The result is streamed back and kept in the App's private cache on your phone until Android clears it, you clear App data, or you uninstall the App. We do not put photos in a cloud photo library, database, or model-training dataset.

Face-match check (the identity gate)

As part of restoration, the service temporarily computes face embeddings and a numeric similarity score comparing the face in the restored image with the original. This lets the App warn you when the result may depict the person inaccurately. The embeddings exist only during that request and are not retained or used to identify you. The score is returned to the App and is not retained by the restoration service.

Purchase information

If you buy a restoration-credit pack, the transaction is handled by Google Play Billing. Our server receives the product ID, an opaque purchase token, order information, purchase region, and purchase status so it can verify the purchase with Google, grant the correct credits, prevent duplicate grants, process refunds or cancellations, and consume an exhausted pack so it can be purchased again. We never receive your Google account email, name, card number, or payment details.

Free-trial count

Heirloom provides three free restorations. To prevent the trial from resetting whenever App data is cleared or the App is reinstalled, the App creates a one-way hash from an Android app-scoped device identifier and sends only that hash to our server. The raw Android identifier is not transmitted. Our server stores the hash and remaining free-credit balance. The hash is not your email, name, advertising ID, or precise location.

App and purchase verification

The App uses Google Play Integrity when it requests a restoration session. Google checks whether the App is Play-installed, recognized, licensed, and running on a device that meets its integrity requirements. Our server receives the resulting integrity verdict and a request-bound verification token. This protects paid restoration compute from modified or unlicensed clients.

Problem reports you choose to send

Every completed result includes a "Report a problem" control. If you use it, we receive the reason you select, optional text you enter, the App version, and limited facts about the result such as whether colorization ran and whether the identity check warned or was unavailable. The original and restored photos are not included. Reports are kept for up to 90 days so we can investigate safety and quality problems.

Basic service data

Cloudflare and Google Cloud may process standard network and service information such as an IP address, request time, response status, and diagnostic logs to deliver, secure, and operate the service. We do not add analytics or advertising tracking and do not use this information to build a user profile.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not store your photos or keep copies after processing.
  • We do not require or create an account, and we do not collect your name or email.
  • We do not request your precise location, contacts, or advertising identifier.
  • We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, or advertising IDs.
  • We do not retain facial templates or use the identity check to identify you.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.

Third-Party Services

Cloudflare

Cloudflare provides the secure gateway that streams each restoration request to our backend and accepts user-submitted problem reports. Photos are not stored in the report database. See Cloudflare's privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Google Play Billing

Purchases are processed by Google Play. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Google Play Integrity

Google Play Integrity verifies that restoration requests come from the recognized, Play-licensed App and an eligible device. It does not give us your Google account email or payment details.

Google Cloud (our backend)

Our restoration service and all five image models run on Google Cloud infrastructure. Files are created only in temporary request storage and deleted when processing ends. Photos are not sent to a separate third-party AI inference provider.

AI Model Training

We do not use your photos to train any AI model — not ours, not anyone's. Your images are used solely to produce the restoration you asked for, and are discarded afterward.

Biometric Data

Heirloom's identity check temporarily converts detected faces into mathematical embeddings solely to compare the original and restored versions within the same request. The service does not retain those embeddings, enroll them in a database, match them against other people, or use them to determine who someone is.

Children's Privacy

Heirloom is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that provided us personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your Rights

Because we don't keep a photo library or user account, there is no persistent photo profile to access or delete. Specifically:

  • Deletion: Your photos are already deleted automatically after each restoration. Clearing App data or uninstalling removes cached files from your device, but does not reset the server-enforced free trial. Contact us about deletion of server-side billing or trial data; deleting an active credit ledger may forfeit unused credits.
  • Access / questions: Email us at any time and we'll help (see Contact).
  • Problem reports: To ask us to find or delete a report before its automatic 90-day expiration, contact us with the approximate submission date and the text you entered.
  • Purchases: Manage or request refunds for your purchase through Google Play.

Security

  • Photos are transmitted to our backend over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • Photos use temporary request storage, are deleted at request end, and are not intentionally written to logs or persistent databases.
  • No account means no password or credential for an attacker to steal.

Regional Availability

Heirloom's initial Google Play launch is available in the United States. Paid packs are accepted only when Google Play reports the purchase region as the United States.
